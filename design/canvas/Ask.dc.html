Derivatives
Why does a derivative represent slope?
Zoom in far enough on a smooth curve and it stops looking curved — the derivative is the slope of the straight line you're left with.
EXPLANATION
Average slope between two points is rise over run. Slide the second point towards the first and that ratio settles on a single number — the slope at the point itself, rather than across a gap.
COMMON MISCONCEPTION
That dy/dx is a fraction you can split apart. It's a limit that happens to be written like one.
